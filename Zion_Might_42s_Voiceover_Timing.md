# The Zion Might — 42-second Voiceover & Audio Structure

## Voiceover script
“Every life is a journey. But every journey needs a path.

The Volume of the Book — The Path of Life takes us back to the beginning, revealing the story of humanity, the coming of Christ, and the Kingdom of God.

Through truth, faith, and transformation, men are formed to discover their purpose, walk the Kingdom journey, and become witnesses in every sphere of life.

From families to communities, and from communities to nations.

Open the Volume of the Book… and discover the path of life.”

## Visual / narration map
- 0.0–4.0s — Fellowship / people studying: “Every life is a journey. But every journey needs a path.”
- 4.0–13.8s — Close/detail + fellowship: Volume of the Book introduction and beginning / humanity / Christ / Kingdom of God.
- 13.8–28.1s — Fellowship develops into wider community: truth, faith, transformation, purpose, Kingdom journey, witnesses.
- 28.1–32.1s — Young people holding the books: “From families to communities…”
- 32.1–36.1s — Older villagers/community holding the books: “…and from communities to nations.”
- 36.1–38.3s — Children/community: begin the invitation to open the book.
- 38.3–42.0s — Book close: “Open the Volume of the Book… and discover the path of life.” with a short musical tail.

## Audio treatment
- Deep male synthetic draft voice used only as a timing/structure reference.
- Voice is compressed and lightly reverberated so it sits forward in the mix.
- Low cinematic drone underneath the narration.
- Very subtle transition hits at major scene changes.
- Soft closing chime when the final book shot appears.
- Voice remains dominant; music is deliberately restrained.

## Important
The visual source was not redesigned or replaced. The original 1920×1080 visuals are retained; the audio is the new layer.
